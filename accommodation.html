Stay Information

Welcome to Vellore

The Organizing Committee of BioMET ’26 warmly welcomes all delegates to Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore, for the conference to be held from 5–7 August 2026. To ensure a comfortable and enjoyable stay, a selection of accommodation options is available in the vicinity of the VIT Vellore campus.

For participants who prefer private or premium accommodation, a curated list of nearby hotels and serviced apartments has been provided. These properties offer a variety of choices to suit different preferences, comfort levels, and budgets, while ensuring convenient access to the conference venue.

Delegates are kindly requested to contact the respective hotels directly for information regarding room availability, tariffs, and reservation procedures. As accommodation arrangements are managed independently by the individual establishments, the BioMET ’26 Organizing Committee is unable to facilitate off-campus bookings or provide transportation services between the hotels and the conference venue.

We strongly encourage participants to review the accommodation options and secure their reservations at the earliest convenience to avoid last-minute availability constraints. Early booking will help ensure a pleasant, comfortable, and seamless experience throughout your participation in BioMET ’26.

We look forward to welcoming you to Vellore and wish you a rewarding and memorable conference experience.
